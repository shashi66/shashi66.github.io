Research

Latent Reasoning

Reasoning in continuous space instead of through explicit tokens. GLR (NeurIPS 2026) frames latent reasoning as a geometric path in the LLM's token-embedding space, anchored by chain-of-thought traces, and yields much shorter generations.

RL-based Post-training

Post-training with reinforcement learning. Using GRPO, we adapt SpeechLLMs on synthetic speech alone and reduce WER by 40% relative to supervised fine-tuning (SLT 2026).

Test-Time Compute Scaling

Trading inference-time compute for accuracy. LARM, a depth-conditioned looped Transformer, reuses one shared block across recurrent passes so that encoder depth can be scaled at inference time.

Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning

FVAE-LoRA (NeurIPS 2025) factorizes task-relevant and residual features in low-rank adapters, improving robustness and performance across image, text, and audio tasks.

Cross-Modal Alignment with Optimal Transport

OTTC is a differentiable sequence-alignment framework based on 1D optimal transport, enabling the model to learn a single alignment and perform ASR in an end-to-end manner.

Multitask & Streaming ASR

Single models that jointly handle transcription, speaker change detection, endpointing, and NER (TokenVerse, EMNLP 2024; TokenVerse++, ASRU 2025), streaming ASR with self-supervised encoders (XLSR-Transducer), and compressed conversational context for LLM-based ASR (SLT 2026).

Currently working on interpretability of cross-modal interactions.
